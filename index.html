A little more about me

Frequently
Asked Questions

More information about my background, products and development approach.

Who is Girish Lade?
I'm Girish Balaso Lade, an independent UI/UX designer, full-stack developer and product builder based in Pune, India. I am the founder of LadeStack, an ecosystem of browser-first developer and productivity tools.
What is LadeStack?
LadeStack is my independent product initiative bringing together software tools for coding, documents, images and everyday productivity. Visit ladestack.in to explore the ecosystem.
What type of products do you build?
My main areas of interest are browser-based developer environments, modern web applications, local-first document tools, creative utilities and AI-assisted development workflows.
What is your educational background?
My academic foundation is in Mechanical Engineering. That background influenced my systems-oriented approach to solving problems and eventually led to my interest in software engineering and digital product design.
What is GB Coder?
GB Coder is a browser-based coding studio designed for creating and previewing web projects. Its development direction includes Monaco-based editing and WebContainer-powered workflows, which are still being improved.
Can I explore your source code?
Yes. You can browse my public repositories, product experiments and available source code on GitHub. Usage permissions depend on each repository's license.
Why do you focus on browser-first tools?
Browser-first applications can reduce setup requirements and make software more accessible. Where appropriate, client-side processing can also reduce the need to send personal files to external servers.
How do you use AI in development?
I explore AI agents for research, implementation, refactoring, debugging and documentation. Architecture, testing, verification and responsible design remain important parts of the process.
Are your products free to use?
My focus is on making useful tools accessible without unnecessary costs. Individual product features, limits and pricing may vary as the ecosystem evolves. Check the relevant product website for current details.
How can I get in touch with you?
You can email me at admin@ladestack.in or connect through my GitHub and LinkedIn profiles. I'm interested in thoughtful product ideas, technical discussions and collaboration opportunities.
